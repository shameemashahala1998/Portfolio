UX / UI Case Study

Problem Statement

Parents struggle to find reliable and comprehensive information about educational institutions,such as curriculum, fees, facilities, and eligibility while also dealing with complex and time-consuming admission processes. The lack of a centralized platform and limited transparency in application status often leads to confusion, frustration, delays, and uninformed decision-making.

Task

Design and develop a user-friendly digital admission platform that enables parents to easily explore comprehensive information about schools and colleges, compare options, and complete their child’s admission process seamlessly.

Solution

we designed a centralized digital admission platform that simplifies the entire admission journeyfrom discovering institutions to completing applications and tracking progress.
